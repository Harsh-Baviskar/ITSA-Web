## What is ITSA and what do we do?

ITSA stands for Information Technology Students' Association.

Embarking on an exhilarating journey across the academic year, our committee orchestrates a symphony of diverse events, captivating workshops, spirited competitions, enlightening webinars, and an array of engaging activities. Through this dynamic blend, we enrich students' lives, expanding their horizons and nurturing a robust knowledge base, igniting their passion for learning and growth.
